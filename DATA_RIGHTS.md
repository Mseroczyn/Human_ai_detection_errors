# Data provenance and redistribution

The original conversations and human judgments are from Cameron R. Jones and
Benjamin K. Bergen's Three-party Turing test project:
https://osf.io/jk7bw/ .
The associated paper is https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2524472123 .

This repository includes a minimal derived table of game identifiers, human
decisions, word counts, consistently pseudonymized participant identifiers,
and newly collected Pangram verdicts and normalized authorship shares. Those
observations allow the statistics to be reproduced without a paid detector
subscription.

The original transcript files are linked rather than mirrored. The source
project's API metadata did not identify a licence when checked on 20 October
2026. Public accessibility alone was not treated as an explicit redistribution
licence. No licence for the original conversations is granted by this repository.

The Pangram measurements were collected with version 4.1 on 19 October 2026.
Only extracted categorical/numeric observations are included. Full PDF reports,
report layouts, logos, account pages and private scan URLs are not included.
Pangram's published terms distinguish user-submitted content from its service
materials; they do not explicitly settle redistribution rights for every form
of generated report:
https://www.pangram.com/terms-of-service .

The code's MIT licence applies to the original Python code. It does not
relicense the original research data, Pangram's software/report designs, or
other third-party materials. Please credit the original study and describe
these results as a separate secondary analysis.
